# Mobile Apps

## Requirements

The iOS app requires iOS 16 or later. The Android app requires Android 10 or later.

## Features

- **Camera upload:** photos and videos are uploaded automatically. By default camera upload only
  runs on Wi-Fi.
- **Offline files:** mark files or folders as available offline.
- **Biometric unlock:** protect the app with Face ID, Touch ID or Android biometrics.

## End-to-end encrypted vaults on mobile

E2E vaults are supported in the mobile apps starting with app version 3.1. The passphrase is
entered on the device and never sent to the server.
