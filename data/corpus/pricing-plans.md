# Pricing and Plans

Driftbox has three plans. Prices are per user and in US dollars.

## Community (free)

- Up to 5 users and 3 vaults.
- Support through the community forum only.
- Up to 3 devices per user.

## Team

- $6 per user per month billed annually, or $7.50 per user per month billed month-to-month.
- Minimum of 3 users.
- Unlimited vaults and unlimited devices per user.
- Email support.
- Registered non-profit organizations get a 20% discount on Team.
- A 30-day free trial of Team is available and does not require a credit card.

## Enterprise

- $14 per user per month, billed annually only.
- Everything in Team, plus SAML single sign-on (SSO), audit log export, immutable snapshots and
  priority support.
- Driftbox Cloud customers on Enterprise receive a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.

SAML SSO is not available on Community or Team. Team customers can use LDAP directory sync
instead.
