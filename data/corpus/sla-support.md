# Support and SLA

## Support channels by plan

- **Community:** community forum, no guaranteed response time.
- **Team:** email support with a first response within 1 business day.
- **Enterprise:** 24/7 support. Severity 1 incidents get a first response within 4 hours.

## Driftbox Cloud uptime SLA (Enterprise)

Quillfeather commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Driftbox Cloud on Enterprise. If monthly uptime
falls below the commitment, customers receive service credits:

| Monthly uptime | Service credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% | 10% of the monthly fee |
| Below 99.0% | 25% of the monthly fee |

The SLA does not apply to self-hosted servers, since Quillfeather does not operate them.
