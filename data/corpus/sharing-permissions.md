# Sharing and Permissions

## Vault roles

Each vault member has one role:

- **Owner** – full control, including deleting the vault and changing retention.
- **Editor** – can add, change and delete files.
- **Viewer** – can read and download files.
- **Uploader** – can only upload files and cannot see existing content. This is useful for
  "file drop" folders, for example collecting documents from clients.

## Public links

Files and folders can be shared with people outside the organization through public links.

- Public links expire after 14 days by default. The maximum expiry is 365 days.
- Links can have a download limit.
- Password-protected links are available on Team and Enterprise.

Administrators can disable public links for a vault entirely.
