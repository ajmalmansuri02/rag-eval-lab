# Data Residency and Compliance

## Regions

Driftbox Cloud customers choose a region when the account is created: Frankfurt (eu-central) or
Oregon (us-west). Customer files and backups stay in the chosen region.

## Compliance

- A GDPR Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available to all Driftbox Cloud customers.
- A SOC 2 Type II report is produced annually and is shared with Enterprise customers under NDA.
- Driftbox Cloud is **not** HIPAA compliant, and Quillfeather does not sign Business Associate
  Agreements (BAAs).

## Data deletion

After an account is closed, customer data is deleted within 30 days and backups are purged
within 60 days.
